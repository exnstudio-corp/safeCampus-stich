Emergency Broadcast Protocol

history Updated 2h ago visibility 1.2k views

The Emergency Broadcast Protocol (EBP) is the primary defensive communication layer within the Guardian Shield ecosystem. It allows campus administrators to push instantaneous, un-dismissible alerts across all mobile devices, digital signage, and connected security nodes during high-priority incidents.

speed

3-Second Latency

Guaranteed delivery to 98.4% of devices in under 3 seconds.

cell_tower

Multi-Channel

Parallel push via SMS, App Notification, and Desktop HUD.

Activation Steps

01

Initialize the Command Core

Access the 'Broadcast' module from the primary navigation. Verify your biometric credentials if prompted. This step ensures that only authorized personnel can initiate campus-wide alerts.

02

Select Threat Level

Choose from pre-defined templates or select 'Custom' for unique scenarios. Threat levels are color-coded: Cyan (Standard), Amber (Caution), and Crimson (Critical).

Note: Templates include "Shelter in Place", "Inclement Weather", and "Facility Lockdown".

03

Geofence Definition

Draw the affected area on the interactive grid. Users entering or leaving this zone will receive automated follow-up instructions based on their GPS coordinates.

warning
emergency

Critical Alerts & Overrides

The 'Critical' alert state overrides Do Not Disturb (DND) and Focus modes on all supported hardware. This is reserved for immediate life-safety threats ONLY.

  • check_circle Audible alarms will trigger at maximum system volume.
  • check_circle Screen brightness will be forced to 100% to ensure readability in all conditions.
  • check_circle Local authorities are automatically patched into the live audio feed.

Post-Broadcast Analysis

Once the incident is resolved, a comprehensive report is generated. This includes heatmaps of user movement during the alert, acknowledgement rates, and response time metrics. This data is critical for refining future emergency responses.
